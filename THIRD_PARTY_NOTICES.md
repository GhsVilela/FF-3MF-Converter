## OrcaSlicer printer profiles and generated templates

The files under `profiles/` are derived from the open-source OrcaSlicer project:

- Project: **OrcaSlicer**
- Source: <https://github.com/OrcaSlicer/OrcaSlicer>
- Versions used:
  - **2.3.0** for the FlashForge AD5X, Adventurer 5M and Adventurer 5M Pro
    templates and resolved profiles.
  - **2.5.0** for the FlashForge Creator 5 and Creator 5 Pro profiles and
    templates.
- License: **AGPL-3.0-or-later**
- Upstream license text: <https://github.com/OrcaSlicer/OrcaSlicer/blob/main/LICENSE>

The target `project_settings.config` templates were generated by running the
open-source OrcaSlicer command-line interface with a small test model and the
corresponding FlashForge machine, process and filament profiles. The machine,
process and filament JSON files were produced by resolving the `inherits`
chains from the same pinned OrcaSlicer releases.

No proprietary Flash Studio code, assets, profiles or screenshots are included.
